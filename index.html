Verification Process

From Synthesis to
Your Lab. Documented.

We don't rely on supplier claims. Every batch goes through a documented multi-step verification process before any shipment leaves our facility.

01
Controlled Sourcing

We source exclusively from vetted synthesis partners with documented quality management systems. No intermediary brokers. Every supplier relationship is direct and traceable.

02
Lyophilization

Compounds are lyophilized immediately after synthesis to remove moisture and maximize shelf stability. This process preserves structural integrity and ensures 2–8°C home storage compatibility.

03
HPLC Purity Analysis

Each batch undergoes High-Performance Liquid Chromatography by an independent accredited laboratory. Purity results reported as actual percentage values — minimum ≥99.0%.

04
COA Issued & Filed

Full Certificate of Analysis compiled with batch number, test date, laboratory, purity %, and identity confirmation. Available on request. Lot numbers tie directly back to this record.

05
24h Dispatch

Verified batches ship within 24 hours of payment confirmation from Philadelphia, PA. Full tracking provided on every order. COA available for any shipment via support@aminoaxis.com.

Direct Support
support@aminoaxis.com
How do I get the Certificate of Analysis for my order?▾
Every batch has a corresponding COA available on request. Email support@aminoaxis.com with your order number and we will provide the full Certificate of Analysis, including the purity percentage, test date, lot number, and the independent laboratory that performed the analysis. You can also browse certificates at aminoaxis.com/certificates.
What payment methods do you accept?▾
We accept all major credit and debit cards as well as Bitcoin (BTC). Bitcoin payments require no account — a new address is generated for each transaction for maximum privacy. All card payments are processed through a secure, encrypted gateway.
How quickly do orders ship?▾
All orders ship within 24 hours of payment confirmation from our Philadelphia, PA facility. You will receive tracking information by email as soon as your order is dispatched. Delivery times vary by destination but most domestic orders arrive within 2–5 business days.
What are Research Stacks?▾
Research Stacks are curated bundles of verified compounds and accessories designed for specific research protocols. They offer savings of up to 15% compared to purchasing individual items. Each bundle is assembled from the same verified inventory as individual purchases — same documentation, same quality standards. Browse available stacks at aminoaxis.com/bundles.
What does "lyophilized" mean and how should I store the product?▾
Lyophilization (freeze-drying) removes moisture from the compound without degrading its structural integrity. This significantly extends shelf life and makes storage simpler. All AminoAxis products are supplied in lyophilized form and should be stored at 2–8°C (standard refrigerator temperature). Reconstitution guidance is available from our support team.
Who are these products intended for?▾
No. All products sold by AminoAxis are intended exclusively for use in accordance with applicable regulations and are intended for laboratory use in accordance with applicable regulations. These products have not been evaluated by the FDA for diagnostic or therapeutic use. Purchasers are solely responsible for compliance with all applicable laws and regulations in their jurisdiction.
